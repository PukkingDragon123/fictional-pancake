WOMBAT OS
HOW TO PLAY
  1. Press P for your phone: the job in hand and where it happens, every wombat and what it is short of, what is in the beds, the weather, the money, and a list of places you can drive to from right here.
  2. Right-click (or press Tab) and a tool tray opens beside the cursor; pick one and it rides with the pointer. The grove is wider than the window - drag bare ground, roll the wheel, or press the arrow keys to pan.
  3. The hooded one tells you what to do next in a speech bubble. Click him and you can actually talk to him — he will repeat the order or answer whatever you ask. Shaz and Groot will talk too.
  4. Till a bed, sow a crop, water it while it grows, and pick it when it glows.
  5. Feed a wombat and it leaves a cube. Drag the cubes into the truck parked at the edge of the clearing.
  6. Click the truck for the map, or open the Places app on your phone and drive from where you stand. Wombat Mart is the corner shop, Groot's Cellar sells seed and garden tools, and the Ritual Site calls the gods.
  7. The hooded one buys every cube she leaves, whatever kind, and pays more for a load than for one. Sell to him where he stands; once he has finished showing you round he moves into a hut at the back of your plot and you sell at the door.
  8. In the mart, swipe the aisles, click what you want into the basket, and pay at the till on the left. One line is half price every visit. By the counter there is a gumball machine at 1 W$ a turn and a lottery at 40 W$ a ticket, and at the far end a cage with three wombats in it — click one to read its papers before you take it home.
