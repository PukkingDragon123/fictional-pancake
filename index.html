HOW TO PLAY
  1. Right-click (or press Tab) to open the tool tray, or press 1–9. The garden is wider than the window: drag bare ground, roll the wheel or use the arrow keys to look around.
  2. Aunt Fern, your neighbour, tells you what to do next in a speech bubble. Click her to chat, or to sell her your cubes.
  3. Cut the weeds with the sickle, send the ants for the old logs, and sow grass. A tidy garden brings a wombat.
  4. Hoe a bed, sow seed on it, water it, and pick it with the hand when it glows.
  5. Feed a wombat and she leaves a cube. Drag the cubes into the truck, then sell them to Aunt Fern. She pays more for a load.
  6. The shovel shapes the land: hold the left button to raise a hill, hold the right button (or Shift) to dig a pond. [ and ] change the brush size.
  7. Click the truck for the map. Wombat Mart has furniture and more wombats and Groot's Cellar has seed and garden things.
  8. Press P for your phone: jobs, your herd, the garden, messages and more.
