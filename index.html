Pawnbroker
The Rite of the Grove
  1. The grove is dead. Clear the weeds and the bugs, then sow moss by dragging — nothing snaps to a grid.
  2. Till a patch, sow a crop on it, water it, and pick it when it glows.
  3. Feed a wombat and it will leave an offering. Pet it to hurry that along.
  4. Stage offerings at the shrine. When a god's demand is met, summon it.
  5. Pawn gold and artifacts for W$. Spend W$ on seed, stock, ground and stone.
  6. The Tree of Knowledge grows fruit as the forest returns. A wombat eats the fruit for you.
  7. At the Rite, stack offerings on the plinth. Catch a cupid for a blessing. Leave before it falls.
