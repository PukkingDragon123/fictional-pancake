How to play

  1. The ground is dead ash. Pick the Censer and sweep some of it away.
  2. Till the bare ground with the trowel, then sow a seed into the soil.
  3. Wombats find ripe crops on their own and eat them. Eating is what brings the moss back.
  4. What they leave is an offering. Click it to store it. Click a wombat with no tool to pet it.
  5. At the Shrine, offerings pay for a rite. Each god you wake blesses the whole grove.
  6. At the Tower, stack offerings on the altar. Height brings pilgrims, pilgrims pay, and the run's goal pays favour.
  7. Fruit comes off crops and buys skills on the root network. Favour buys the gods' artifacts.
  8. Pair two grown wombats in the Warren. Joeys inherit their parents' traits and coats.
