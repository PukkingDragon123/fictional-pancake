How to play

  1. Pick a feed, click a wombat. It digests, then leaves a cube.
  2. Click with no feed selected to pet. Petting speeds digestion and keeps them happy.
  3. Happy wombats leave premium cubes, worth double.
  4. At the Big Top, drop cubes on the pedestal. Height brings the crowd, the crowd pays.
  5. Every five cubes you pick a perk. Cash out before the tower topples.
  6. Compost cubes at the tree to grow it. Growing it earns acorns and opens new boughs.
