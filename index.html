HOW TO PLAY
  1. Momo gives you jobs. Click her to chat or sell cubes.
  2. Tools: 1–0. Buy them at Wombat Mart (click the truck).
  3. Cut weeds, clear logs, sow grass. A wombat comes!
  4. Grow veg, feed wombats, collect their cube poop.
  5. Buy furniture from Captain Kirk, then BUILD (B) a poop factory. R rotates.
  6. Explore Still Lake, Blackwood and Fern Gully on the map (M).
