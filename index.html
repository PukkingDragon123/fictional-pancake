HOW TO PLAY
  1. You start with your two hands and W$300. Jim gives you his old sickle, and each job he teaches unlocks the next tool. Buy them at Wombat Mart: click the truck to drive there.
  2. Jim, the old caretaker, tells you your next job in a speech bubble. Click him to chat, or to sell him your cubes.
  3. Pick a tool on the toolbar along the bottom, or press 1–0. Locked tools show a padlock; unlocked ones you have not bought show their price. An arrow points at your current job. Drag bare ground, roll the wheel or use the arrow keys to look around.
  4. Cut the weeds with the sickle, have the ants take the old logs, and sow grass. A tidy garden brings a wombat.
  5. Hoe a bed, sow seed on it, water it, and pick it with the hand when it glows.
  6. Feed a wombat and she leaves a cube. Put cubes in the truck and sell them to Jim, or spread one on a bed with the Poo Scoop and it grows twice as fast.
  7. The shovel paints the ground: hold the left button to dig down, the right button (or Shift) to heap up a hill. Water a hole with the can and it fills into a pond; water a trench and it runs like a creek. [ and ] change the brush size.
  8. Press P for your phone once you have one. It comes with Jobs, Messages and Settings; get more apps from the App Store.
