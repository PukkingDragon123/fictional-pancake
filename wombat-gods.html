The Rite of the Grove
  1. The grove is dead. Cut the weeds, catch the bugs, have the ants haul off the wrecks, then sow grass by dragging.
  2. Finish the list and a wombat walks in.
  3. Till a bed, sow a crop, water it while it grows, and pick it when it glows.
  4. Feed a wombat and it leaves a cube. Drag the cubes into the truck, or call the truck to you.
  5. Click the seed in the middle of the plot for the Tree of Life. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom.
  6. Click the signpost for the map. The Ritual Site is where gods are called; the Great Stack is where offerings are stacked.
  7. The store sells seed, stock, ground and stone. Swipe the shelves, fill the basket, pay at the counter.
