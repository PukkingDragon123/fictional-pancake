The Rite of the Grove
  1. The grove is wider than the window. Drag bare ground, roll the wheel, or press the arrow keys to pan the camera.
  2. Cut the weeds, have the ants haul off the wrecks, then sow grass by dragging. Finish the list and a wombat walks in.
  3. Till a bed, sow a crop, water it while it grows, and pick it when it glows.
  4. Feed a wombat and it leaves a cube. Drag the cubes into the truck, or call the truck to you.
  5. Click the seed in the middle of the plot for the Tree of Life. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom.
  6. Click the signpost for the map. Wombat Mart sells seed and stock, the Ritual Site calls the gods, the Great Stack pays for a tall pile of offerings.
  7. In the mart, swipe the aisles, click what you want into the basket, and pay at the counter.
