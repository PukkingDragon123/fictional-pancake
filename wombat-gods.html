HOW TO PLAY
  1. Right-click (or press Tab) and a tool tray opens beside the cursor; pick one and it rides with the pointer. The grove is wider than the window - drag bare ground, roll the wheel, or press the arrow keys to pan.
  2. The hooded one tells you what to do next in a speech bubble; click him to hear it again. Finish an order and he pays you in coin.
  3. Till a bed, sow a crop, water it while it grows, and pick it when it glows.
  4. Feed a wombat and it leaves a cube. Drag the cubes into the truck parked at the edge of the clearing.
  5. Click the truck for the map. Wombat Mart sells seed and stock, the Ritual Site calls the gods, and the Great Stack is where the money is.
  6. At the Great Stack, drop poop on the plinth. Every cube pays when it lands and the higher the tower goes the more it pays. The crowd tips by the second for as long as it stands — and it stays standing while you are away. Nothing is ever lost: a cube that falls off is caught and handed back.
  7. In the mart, swipe the aisles, click what you want into the basket, and pay at the counter. One line is half price every visit, and the prize machine by the till takes 250 W$ a go.
